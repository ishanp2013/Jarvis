                        Routines

                          Memory and privacy

                            Microsoft: Outlook.com mail, Calendar and To Do

                            Official Microsoft sign-in (Microsoft Graph). You need a free app registration first; see SETUP.md.

                            Google: Gmail, Calendar, Tasks and Drive

                            Official Google sign-in. You need your own free OAuth client ("Desktop app") from Google Cloud first; see SETUP.md.

                            Google Classroom (school account)

                            Read-only: your classes, classwork with due dates, and announcements. Sign in with your school Google account. It uses the same OAuth client as above. First enable the Google Classroom API in your Google Cloud project, and add your school address under Test users. Some schools block outside apps; Google then says so, and only the school can allow it.

                            GitHub

                            Notifications, your repos, assigned issues and review requests, and opening issues. Uses a personal access token you create on GitHub (Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens).

                            Classic Outlook (desktop)

                            Reads accounts already set up in classic Outlook, including Gmail over IMAP. No cloud setup needed. Classic Outlook must be installed and signed in.

                            Discord (bot)

                            Uses your own Discord bot, invited only to the servers you choose. It can't read your personal DMs.

                            Your phone (KDE Connect)

                            Find your phone, open apps and links on it, and see its notifications. Pair it in the KDE Connect app on both devices (Microsoft Store and Play Store), and turn on Notification sync on the phone. Unlocking the phone, or pressing Call/Send in its apps, isn't possible: Android doesn't allow it.

                            checking…

                            Notion

                            Search, read and add notes. Create an internal integration at notion.so/my-integrations, then share the pages you want Jarvis to see with it (page menu → Connections).

                            Slack

                            Read and post in channels through your own Slack app's bot (api.slack.com/apps → OAuth & Permissions → Bot token, with channels:read, channels:history, chat:write, users:read). Invite the bot to each channel.

                            Telegram

                            A bot you make with @BotFather. It reads messages sent to the bot and replies to people who have messaged it first. It can't use your personal account.

                            Smart home (Home Assistant)

                            Lights, switches, fans, covers, climate and scenes through your own Home Assistant. Locks and alarms are never touched.

                            Games, YouTube and YouTube Music

                            No setup. "Play Rocket League" launches installed Steam and Epic games; "play Dracula on YouTube" or "on YouTube Music" opens the top result in your browser.

                            WhatsApp

                            There is no official way to read personal WhatsApp chats. Jarvis sends to saved contacts through the WhatsApp app (or only fills the message in, if "Ask before sending" is on).

                            Contacts: save a number so "send hi to Sam on WhatsApp" opens that chat directly. Numbers stay on this PC.
